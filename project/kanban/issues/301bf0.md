## What to build
From the 2026-10-13 review of e2fb8a. The packaged `wcmp` crate's unit tests still cannot compile.

- `exclude = ["/tests/*", "!/tests/support/"]` works as intended: the `.crate` keeps `tests/support/backend.rs`.
- But `tests/support/backend.rs` and `src/runtime_layer.rs` name `wcmp_wasm_core_wasmtime`, `wcmp_wasm_core_wasmi` and `wcmp_wasm_core_web`. These are path-only dev-dependencies in the root `Cargo.toml`. Cargo drops a dev-dependency with no `version` when it publishes ("only dev-dependencies that specify a `version` will be included", Cargo reference, "Specifying dependencies").
- So `cargo test` on the `.crate` fails with unresolved crates. The comment at `rust/wcmp/Cargo.toml` ("which the crate's own unit tests include") suggests they work.

Owner decision: either
- give those three workspace dependencies a `version`, as `wcmp-wasm-core` and `wcmp-macros` have, and prove the packaged unit tests compile; or
- go back to `exclude = ["/tests/"]` and say in the comment that the packaged unit tests are not supported.
