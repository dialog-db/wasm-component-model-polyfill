## What to build

Follow-up from the first review of the PDD027 series, which this series recorded as a deviation rather than changed. Decide whether the handle and reader methods should take the store the way Wasmtime's do.

- **Today:** these methods take `&mut StoreContext<'_, D>`:
  - `ResourceAny::resource_drop`, `try_from_resource`, `try_into_resource`, and `try_into_resource_dynamic`;
  - `Resource::try_from_resource_any` and `try_into_resource_any`;
  - the same on `ResourceDynamic`;
  - `StreamReader::new` and `FutureReader::new`, and the `try_into_*_any` conversions.

  A host that holds a `Store` writes `any.resource_drop(&mut store.as_context_mut())`. The polyfill has no public `AsContextMut`, and these follow the precedent of `StreamReader::new`.
- **Wasmtime:** takes `impl AsContextMut` (`crates/wasmtime/src/runtime/component/resources/any.rs:165`, `host.rs:286` at `v49.0.0-rc.1`), so Wasmtime code passes `&mut store` and does not compile against the polyfill.

This needs the owner's decision. Either add a public trait that both `Store` and `StoreContext` implement, and take `impl` of it everywhere Wasmtime takes `impl AsContextMut`, or keep the current form and record it as a design decision.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The owner has decided, and the decision is recorded.
- [ ] If the trait lands, every method above takes it, and a test passes `&mut store` and a `StoreContext` to each, on both targets.
- [ ] `lint` passes, and `tests all` is green on both targets with the conformance summary unchanged.
