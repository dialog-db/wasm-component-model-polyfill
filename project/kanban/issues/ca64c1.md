## What to build
Prove, and fix where a proof fails, that the stackful lift runs under a provider on both targets.

The stackful directives:
- Wasmtime corpus: `stackful.wast`, `drop-waitable-set-stackful.wast`, `task-deletion.wast`, and the four stackful directives of `task-return-traps.wast` (93, 106, 121, 138).
- Component Model corpus: `sync-barges-in.wast:323`, `values/variants.wast:186`, and seven directives of `big-interleaving-test.wast` (912, 973, 1295, 1508, 1623, 1640, 1651).

The cascade lines of each owned file go with it. The two `subtask.cancel` directives of `big-interleaving-test.wast` (1664, 1675) fail for another cause; they keep that cause and stay in the shared list.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With a provider, `stackful.wast`, `drop-waitable-set-stackful.wast`, `task-deletion.wast`, `sync-barges-in.wast`, and `values/variants.wast` pass whole on both targets.
- [ ] With a provider, the seven stackful directives of `big-interleaving-test.wast` and the four of `task-return-traps.wast` pass on both targets, with the message "async-lifted export failed to produce a result" where the file expects it.
- [ ] `big-interleaving-test.wast` 1664 and 1675 keep their `subtask.cancel` cause in the shared list.
- [ ] Each stackful directive that fails without a provider sits in `expected-failures.no-provider.txt`, set by a live run.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green in all four states. A directive that starts passing under a provider leaves the shared list. A line that still fails without a provider sits in `expected-failures.no-provider.txt` with the stack-switch reason. Every line whose reason this card changes is refreshed from a live run.
