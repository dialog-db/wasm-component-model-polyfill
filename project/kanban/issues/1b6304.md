## What to build
In the vendored `rust/vendor/js_wasm_runtime_layer` (patch 7 in `PATCHES.md`), `src/lib.rs` around line 479 inserts a `FuncInner` into `StoreInner::funcs` (a slab) on every funcref conversion, and the crate has no removal path. A prepared call converts three funcrefs (two in prepare, one in the synchronous start), so a browser store grows without bound in proportion to the number of guest-to-guest calls. Give the conversion a lifetime that ends with the call, or reuse the entry for a funcref the store has already seen, and record the change in `PATCHES.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Repeated prepared calls in the browser do not grow the store's function slab; a web test proves the slab length after N calls equals the length after one.
- [ ] `PATCHES.md` records the change under patch 7 or a new entry.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green on both targets with the conformance summary unchanged.
