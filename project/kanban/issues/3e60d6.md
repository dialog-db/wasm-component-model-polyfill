## What to build
The flake's `lib.sandbox` call lists two `extraRepos` (`Git/github.com/WebAssembly/component-model` and `Git/github.com/bytecodealliance/wasmtime`) and `project/kanban/.dispatch-instructions.md` tells every implementor and reviewer to read `definitions.py` and Wasmtime's sources there. On a fresh launch they are present (a reviewer on card `0f9fdd` noted the copies carry no git metadata). After `sandbox stop` and a resume through `sandbox prompt`, the implementor of card `ebb8fc` reported that `~/Git` was absent from the VM entirely, so it could not read `unpack_event` or `func/host.rs` while fixing an alignment finding and worked from the polyfill's own conventions instead. The implementor of card `553bb9` reported, on the same kind of resume, that its workspace had been re-seeded onto a different dispatch seed than the one its branch was delivered from and had to `git reset --hard` onto the delivered lineage. A third instance of the re-seed: the implementor of card `f4ac39`, resumed for a one-line revision, found its workspace on a fresh seed over host tip `293bbac` with its two commits replayed, could not push to its delivered branch without a force-push, and had to push an additive `-reseeded` ref that the orchestrator fetched from the instance mirror by hand. Find out what a resume re-provisions and what it drops: whether `extraRepos` are staged into the scratch volume once at first launch and lost when a resume re-seeds it, or staged to a path the resume does not restore. Make a resume leave the reference checkouts in place, and make it not move the workspace off the branch the instance delivered. Prove both with a named instance that is started, stopped, and prompted again. If the fix belongs in the katsuobushi library rather than this flake, say exactly what to change there and file the change upstream; record on this card what this repository can do in the meantime (for example, tell a resumed agent in the resume prompt how to fetch the two commits from `codeload.github.com`, which the allowlist reaches).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] After `sandbox stop` and `sandbox prompt` on a named instance, `~/Git/github.com/WebAssembly/component-model` and `~/Git/github.com/bytecodealliance/wasmtime` are present in the VM.
- [ ] After the same resume, the workspace checkout is at the tip the instance last pushed, not a fresh dispatch seed.
- [ ] The cause and the fix, or the upstream filing, are recorded on this card.
