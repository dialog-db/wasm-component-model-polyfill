## What to build
The lifecycle rule of a host end is Wasmtime's. `close` on `StreamReader<T>` and `FutureReader<T>` drops the readable end through a store context, which completes a pending guest write with the dropped result and lets a later write see it at once; `close_with` does the same through an accessor inside a poll. A reader the host holds must end through `pipe` or `close`: one dropped without either leaks its slot until the store drops, and a guest that writes to it waits forever. `guard` wraps a reader in `GuardedStreamReader<T, D>` or `GuardedFutureReader<T, D>` with the accessor it holds, and the guard closes on drop; a guard dropped outside a poll of its store cannot reach the store, so its end leaks the way an unclosed reader does. Dropping the store (`src/store/store/`) drops every shared record, every end, and every producer and consumer without polling them, which extends the store-drop rule of PDD018.

## Notes from earlier cards
- Host ends live in scheduler maps (producers from bd6ee3, consumers from 34e611), and their wakers are released on release, on drop, and after an `Accessor::with` failure (`release_host_writer`/`release_host_reader`, `host_end_wakers`). Closing a host end and dropping the store must release every one of those, and each release path needs a test.
- A reader or writer carries no store identity; cross-store use is an undetected host error, as in Wasmtime. Do not add a store check here unless the close contract needs one, and say so if it does.
- Card d05670 (`Val::Stream`/`Val::Future`, close on the untyped ends) runs in parallel from the same base; whichever lands second merges the other.
- Do not edit `project/design/`; say in your report where PDD021 disagrees with the code.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `close` completes a guest's pending write with the dropped result, and the guest's next write completes with it at once, proved by a repository test.
- [ ] A reader dropped without `close` leaves a guest write pending until the store drops, proved by a test.
- [ ] A guard closes on drop inside a poll, proved by a test.
- [ ] Dropping the store drops every record and every producer and consumer without polling them, proved by a test that counts the drops.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green on both targets.
