## What to build

PDD026, "HTTP on Both Ends". One Rust implementation of a subset of `wasi:http/types@0.3.0` that serves both contexts, linked with the polyfill's `Linker`.

- The subset is what the authoring library calls on the client side and on the handler side: fields, requests, responses, and their bodies as streams.
- Conversions between the subset and the browser's `Request`, `Response`, and `Headers`, for use by the page's `fetch` client and the service worker's `fetch` listener.
- Every function outside the subset returns an error, so a change in Zena's output shows as a failure and not as a silent wrong answer.

This card does not wire `wasi:http/client` or a handler. Later cards do.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A component that builds a request with a method, headers, and a text body, and reads them back, gets the same values on the page and in the service worker.
- [ ] A request and a response convert to the browser's types and back with method, status, headers, and body intact.
- [ ] A call outside the subset returns an error.
- [ ] `tests all` and `lint` pass.
