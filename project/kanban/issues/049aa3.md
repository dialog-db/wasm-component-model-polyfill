## What to build

Wasmtime 50 follows spec #726 (`437161af0`). An async `subtask.cancel` of a
callback task that is parked after `YIELD` runs the callee inside the cancel.
The callee gets the task-cancelled event, and the cancel answers 4 when the
callee confirms. Before, the cancel answered BLOCKED. The canceller resumes at
the first suspend or exit of the callee, also when the callee switches to the
thread of another task.

The polyfill does not wake these callbacks, because Wasmtime 49 did not
(`executor/cancel.rs:44-56`, `:390-405`). PDD023 says that the polyfill follows
when Wasmtime wakes them.

One case stays a departure. A WAIT callback whose set already holds an event
is not woken, because Wasmtime 50 does not wake it (`concurrent.rs:5615`). The
spec wakes it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A cancel of a callback task parked after `YIELD` runs the callee inside
      the cancel and answers 4 when the callee confirms, on both targets.
- [ ] The canceller resumes at the first suspend or exit of the callee, also
      after a switch to the thread of another task.
- [ ] `baseline_cancel.rs` expects the new answer, and `cancel.rs` and PDD023
      state the WAIT case as a departure.
