## What to build
`wasmtime-environ` 48 compiles a fused adapter whose lift and lower belong to one component instance, or to an instance and its ancestor, to an unconditional `cannot enter component instance` trap. Wasmtime removed that rule in #14146 ("align multithreading and trap behavior with CM spec", 2026-08-31), which ships in Wasmtime 49, and the vendored corpus (`wasmtime/adapter.wast:98`, "valid, but odd") expects such a component to instantiate. The polyfill records the line as a `substrate` failure until it moves.

When `wasmtime` 49, `wasmtime-environ` 49, and `wasmtime_runtime_layer` 49 are published (49 is at `49.0.0-rc.1` on 2026-09-15 and the runtime layer has no 49 yet), bump the three in `[workspace.dependencies]`, follow the translator's API changes (#14146 also reworked the `task_may_block` global and the sync-call intrinsics an adapter imports, so the executor's intrinsics need a matching review), re-baseline the corpus, and remove the `wasmtime/adapter.wast:98` line. The test `it_lowers_a_lifted_function_of_the_same_component` in `tests/baseline_composition.rs` tolerates both outcomes so it flips without an edit.
