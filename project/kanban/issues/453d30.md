## What to build

PDD026, "The Drawer" (the read-only part) and "The Two Contexts: Messages".

- The drawer is a panel at the side of the page, in plain Rust and HTML, not an element. It lists every element and every route. For each one it shows the Zena source in a text area, the time of the last compile and the last instantiation, the diagnostics after a failed compile, and for an element the count of connected elements and of instances.
- **Messages.** The service worker sends `route-compiled` after each compile of a route (pattern, compile time, instantiate time, diagnostics of a failure) and `route-trapped` after a route traps. The page sends `routes-status` when the drawer opens, and the service worker answers with the last `route-compiled` of each route.
- The drawer marks a tag whose `Store` trapped, and a route that trapped.

Editing comes in the next two cards.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The drawer shows timings. After the page starts, the drawer shows a compile time and an instantiate time for every element. After the first request to each route, it shows the same for that route.
- [ ] A route compiles again after the service worker stops. A test stops the service worker and sends a request. The drawer shows a new compile for that route.
- [ ] One instance serves every element of a tag. A test adds fifty elements of one tag and then removes forty. The drawer shows one instance throughout, and it shows fifty and then ten connected elements.
- [ ] The drawer lists four elements and two routes, each with its source.
- [ ] `tests all` and `lint` pass.
