## What to build
`Val` in `src/value/val.rs` gains `Stream(StreamAny)` and `Future(FutureAny)`, so the untyped entries of the linker (`func_new`, `func_new_concurrent`) and `Func::call` carry a stream or a future as they carry a resource. Each `Any` type (one public type per module) holds the readable end and its payload type, offers `close` through a store context, and converts to and from the typed reader: `StreamReader<T>::try_into_stream_any` and `try_from_stream_any`, and the future pair, check that `T` projects to the payload type. Lifting a readable end out of a guest through an untyped call yields the `Any` value and the guest's entry is gone; lowering one into a guest checks its payload type against the guest's and inserts a readable end. Wasmtime holds the two types to close and typed conversion at this version and has stated that it will let a host read and write them without naming the type; the polyfill follows that shape when it lands, and an untyped read or write stays out of scope.
