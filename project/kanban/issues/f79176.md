## What to build
Work through the expected-failures lines categorized `trap-message`: emit the trap messages Wasmtime uses (`unknown handle index N`, `discriminant N out of range [0..M)`, `string pointer/length out of bounds of memory`, `invalid utf-8`, `realloc return: beyond end of memory`, `realloc return: result not aligned`, `string pointer not aligned to 2`, `cannot leave component instance`) and add the checks the messages imply: realloc result bounds and alignment, UTF-16 pointer alignment, and a variant discriminant range check. Route each through `AbiCause` variants rather than free text where a variant exists.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every `trap-message` line whose directive the polyfill otherwise passes is removed from the list.
- [ ] `assert_trap` directives in `values/realloc.wast` and `values/strings.wast` pass.
- [ ] No message is matched by substring where the corpus expects the exact Wasmtime wording.
