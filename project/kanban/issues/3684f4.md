## What to build

Follow-up from the second review of the PDD027 series. `TypedFunc::call` and `call_concurrent` take borrowed parameters, as Wasmtime's do.

- **Today:** `TypedFunc<P, R>` requires `P: 'static` (`rust/wcmp/src/instance/typed_func.rs`, the `impl` bounds). Wasmtime's does not (`crates/wasmtime/src/runtime/component/func/typed.rs:69-71, 167-173` at `v49.0.0-rc.1`).
- **Consequence:** the lower-only forms fc6b5d added (`&str`, `&[T]`, `&T`) cross only as `&'static` borrows, which is what every test uses.
- **Age:** the bound predates the series.

Relax the bound on `call`. If `call_concurrent` needs a `'static` future, keep the bound there only, and say why in its docs.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A typed call passes a `&str` and a `&[u32]` borrowed from a local `String` and `Vec`, on both targets.
- [ ] Any bound that stays is documented, with the reason.
- [ ] `lint` passes, and `tests all` is green on both targets with the conformance summary unchanged.
