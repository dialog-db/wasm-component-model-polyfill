## What to build
Tidy the Rust partner build of the Zena scenarios (landed as `2c71c4b6a`), from the independent review of card 8ba35a. None of this blocked the card.

- **Store paths in the partner bytes.** `tests/zena/partner.sh:68` remaps only the work directory, so both partner `.wasm` files embed `/nix/store/...-rust-default-1.96.0` and `...-vendor-cargo-deps`, and `zena-partners`/`zena-scenarios` keep both as runtime references. `tests/corpus/fixtures/build.sh:76` also remaps the sysroot and registry; do the same.
- **An automated check that the partner ignores the pin.** The evidence today is manual. The reviewer called the flake's outputs function with a `zena` input that throws on any use: `zena-partners.drvPath` stayed identical, while `zena-scenarios` threw. Pure evaluation took about 12 s. Make that a flake check.
- **Failure modes.** A partner in a scenario without `wit/` fails at evaluation with a Nix-internal `lib.fileset.unions` error (`flake.nix:324`) instead of a layout message. The checks at `partner.sh:49` omit `wit/`, and its cargo-lock check can never fire because `vendorCargoDeps` (`flake.nix:329`) reads the lock at evaluation time. There are no build-check cases for `partner.sh` or for the name collision at `flake.nix:368`.
- **World choice.** Each partner hardcodes `world:` in `generate!` (for example `exporter/src/lib.rs:5-8`); only convention ties it to the directory name. The two partners carry identical 326-line locks.

- **A partner's own WIT.** PDD024 lets a partner declare its world in the scenario's WIT or in its own, but `rustPartners` (`flake.nix`, about lines 322-327, fileset of the partner plus the scenario's `wit/`) and `partner.sh` (`cp -R $scenario/wit`) require the scenario's `wit/` and ignore a partner's own. A partners-only scenario without `wit/` fails at evaluation. Accept a `wit/` inside the partner's directory. (From the review of card 6d91fb, finding b.)
- **Build-check gap.** No case covers a scenario whose only subdirectory has no `cargo-manifest.toml` (for example `wit/` alone); `build.sh:58` handles it by inspection. (6d91fb finding d.)

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The partner `.wasm` files hold no `/nix/store` path, and `zena-partners` has no runtime reference to the toolchain or the vendored crates.
- [ ] A flake check fails if the partner build reads the `zena` input.
- [ ] A partner in a scenario without `wit/` fails with a layout message, and `partner.sh` has build-check cases for its failure modes.
- [ ] A partner whose `wit/` sits in its own directory builds and links.
- [ ] `lint` passes.
