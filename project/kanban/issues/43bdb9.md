## What to build

Wasmtime 50 charges hostcall fuel when it lifts a `Val` (`c2741c9bb`,
GHSA-jqpg-j7w6-42pr). It charges one `Val` for each record or tuple field, for
each variant, option, or result payload, and the length of each field, case,
or flag name. The polyfill charges only lists, maps, and strings
(`abi/lift.rs:53`, `:548`, `:826`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The charges match `values.rs` at `v50.0.0`, in memory lifts and in flat
      lifts.
- [ ] A test shows a record lift refused at the budget on both targets, with
      no fuel spent.
