## What to build
`enter_sync_call` in `src/executor/intrinsics.rs` (around line 228 at `577d2a0`) returns early without holding the may-not-suspend flag when the callee's lift is `async`. Wasmtime's rule, stated in the header of `tests/corpus/wasmtime/thread-transparency/blocks.wast`, is that a thread-transparent adapter chain (`$Outer -> $Mid -> $Inner`) clears `task_may_block` for the whole chain, so `$Inner`'s synchronously-lifted `async`-typed export that really blocks on `waitable-set.wait` over an empty set traps with "cannot block a synchronous task before returning". At the tip the chain reaches `$Inner`, its wait parks instead of trapping, nothing fills the set, and the driver fails with the deadlock cause; `blocks.wast:69` records that as a deferred-feature line. Read the reference's `canon_lift` and `Task.enter` for how a synchronous caller's may-not-suspend state reaches an `async`-typed callee through a sync call, and Wasmtime's `enter_guest_sync_call` in `crates/wasmtime/src/runtime/component/concurrent.rs` for the `do_not_suspend` rule, and make the enter intrinsic hold the flag for an `async`-typed callee the way the sources do, restoring it on exit. `blocks.wast` then passes whole and its line leaves the expected-failure list. Check whether `cm/async/dont-block-start.wast` and the other thread-transparency files change state, and regenerate their reasons from a live run if so.
