## What to build
Fill in the parts of the subtask record (`src/concurrency/subtask.rs`, `src/concurrency/subtask_state.rs`, `src/concurrency/task_tables.rs`) that the asynchronous lower observes. The record starts in `STARTING`, moves to `STARTED` when the callee has read its parameters, and to `RETURNED` when the callee has produced its result; the two cancelled states are resolved states that only the failure path PDD018 has reaches here, and the cancel-requested flag stays unused. The record enters the caller's handle table only when the lower returns with a state other than `RETURNED`, with indices from the caller instance's allocator in call order, and a call that resolves before the lower returns leaves no entry. A subtask holds one pending event slot (`src/concurrency/event_slot.rs`): the scheduler fills it when the subtask resolves, and also when the subtask starts if the caller already holds a handle, which is the case of a callee the gate held and later let through. Delivery reads the subtask's state at that moment, so a `STARTED` that was never delivered reads as `RETURNED`; the event is the triple of the subtask code (1), the handle index, and the state. Delivery of a resolved state marks the resolution as delivered and releases every handle the caller lent for the call; a synchronous lower delivers the resolution as it returns. Build `subtask.drop`: map `Trampoline::SubtaskDrop` in `src/executor/translate.rs` to a spec and a trampoline that takes a handle index and traps when the index does not name a subtask (with Wasmtime's message), when the resolution was not delivered (`WaitableCause::SubtaskNotResolved`, with Wasmtime's message), and when the instance's may-leave flag is clear (`TaskCause::CannotLeave`); otherwise it removes the entry. A guest callee's task record leaves the store once its implicit thread has exited and its entry is gone; a host task's record leaves the store with its entry. `waitable-set.wait` and `waitable-set.poll` of PDD019 deliver the subtask event and `waitable.join` moves a subtask between sets, and none of them changes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A `STARTED` that was never delivered reads as `RETURNED` at delivery, proved by a repository test.
- [ ] `subtask.drop` after the resolution was delivered removes the entry; before delivery it traps with Wasmtime's message; on an index that is not a subtask it traps; from a realloc it fails with the cannot-leave cause, each proved by a test.
- [ ] `drop-host.wast` passes and its lines leave the list: a host subtask whose future completed but whose event was not delivered cannot be dropped.
- [ ] The `subtask.drop` component of `task-builtins.wast` instantiates and its line leaves the list.
- [ ] A host task's record leaves the store when its entry is dropped, and a guest callee's task record leaves once its thread has exited and its entry is gone, proved by tests that read the store's records.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green on both targets, and any async directive that starts passing has its line removed from the expected-failure list.
