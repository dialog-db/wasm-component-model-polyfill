## What to build

Confirm, then report upstream, that WebKitGTK 2.54.1 leaves the global
`SharedArrayBuffer` undefined on a cross-origin isolated page, while the
buffer of a shared `WebAssembly.Memory` there is a `SharedArrayBuffer` by
its brand.

The WebKitGTK lane (1429ec) found it: in MiniBrowser through WebKitWebDriver,
on a page `wasm-bindgen-test-runner` serves with
`Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin` and
`Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp`, `crossOriginIsolated` is
`true`, `typeof SharedArrayBuffer` is `"undefined"`, and
`Object.prototype.toString` of a shared memory's buffer gives
`[object SharedArrayBuffer]`. The polyfill does not depend on the global;
`rust/wcmp-wasm-core-web/src/lib.rs` tells a host about it.

First check that it is WebKit's and not MiniBrowser's or the automation
session's: open the same page in Epiphany with the headers, and look for
an existing bug on bugs.webkit.org. The owner files the issue.

Proposed issue text (WebKit Bugzilla, component JavaScriptCore or WebKitGTK):

> **SharedArrayBuffer constructor not exposed on a cross-origin isolated
> page in WebKitGTK 2.54.1**
>
> On a page served with `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin` and
> `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp`, `self.crossOriginIsolated`
> is `true`, and `new WebAssembly.Memory({ initial: 1, maximum: 1, shared:
> true }).buffer` is a shared buffer (`Object.prototype.toString` gives
> `[object SharedArrayBuffer]`), but `typeof SharedArrayBuffer` is
> `"undefined"`, so `buffer instanceof SharedArrayBuffer` throws or is
> false. Expected: the constructor is exposed on a cross-origin isolated
> page, as in Safari and Chromium. Seen in MiniBrowser 2.54.1 on Linux,
> headless Weston, through WebKitWebDriver.
