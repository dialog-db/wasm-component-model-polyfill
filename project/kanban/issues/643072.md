## What to build

Wasmtime 50 compiles an adapter whose lift and lower are in one instance to a
trap with "cannot enter component instance" (`fact/trampoline.rs:117-127` at
`v50.0.0`). Wasmtime says this is not spec conformant and plans to reconcile
it with the spec. The spec at `5b724da` allows the call.

The polyfill gets its adapters from `wasmtime-environ`, so it inherits the
trap. To keep the call working, the polyfill must fork `wasmtime-environ` or
compile its own adapters, because the translator never exposes the dataflow
graph that an adapter needs. The owner chose to follow Wasmtime 50 for now.

The polyfill records the trap as a departure from the spec. The reason is that
the adapter compiler forces it.

This card lands in the same series as 2397ff. The workspace does not build or pass on Wasmtime 50 without it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `tests/baseline_composition.rs` asserts the trap on both targets, and
      the store is poisoned afterwards.
- [ ] `notes/differences-from-wasmtime.md` or `notes/known-limits.md` states
      the departure and its reason.
- [ ] The comments in `Cargo.toml` and `concurrency/scheduler.rs` that say
      the call works are corrected.
