## What to build
`wasmtime/adapter.wast:98` is the corpus's one remaining `defect`. The component lifts a core function and then lowers that same component function back into a core function (`canon lift` followed by `canon lower` of the result in one component). The test says "valid, but odd" and expects instantiation to succeed; the polyfill's instantiation fails with a substrate trap at offset `0x62`, so something runs at instantiation time that Wasmtime does not run. Diagnose (the fused adapter between the lift and the lower, its instance-flag checks, or the executor's initializer order are the candidates) and fix.

Parity: the executor is shared; the fix applies to both targets and the directive passes in both lanes.

## Review notes
Diagnosis (2026-09-15): the trap is `cannot enter component instance`, raised by the fused adapter the translator emits, which is `i32.const 17; call trap; unreachable` and nothing else. `wasmtime-environ` 48 compiles every adapter whose lift and lower belong to the same instance (or to an ancestor) to that unconditional trap, to make reentrance through an adapter impossible. Wasmtime removed the rule in #14146 (2026-08-31, "align multithreading and trap behavior with CM spec"), which is in the 49 release train and in the vendored corpus commit, so the corpus expects the instantiation to succeed. Nothing in the polyfill runs at instantiation that Wasmtime does not; the difference is the pinned translator. Wasmtime 49 is at `49.0.0-rc.1` and `wasmtime_runtime_layer` 49 is not published, so the line is re-categorized as `substrate` with that reason, and the bump is filed as its own card.
