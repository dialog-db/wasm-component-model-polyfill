## What to build
`Func::call_concurrent` (`src/instance/func.rs:298-303`) returns `Poll::Pending` from its `poll_fn` without registering the caller's waker, on the stated assumption that the driver around it polls it again after every turn. `TypedFunc::call_concurrent` shares the path (`src/instance/typed_func.rs:116-124`). The assumption holds only when the closure's future is polled straight through. Inside a `run_concurrent` closure, any waker-gated combinator polls a child again only after that child's waker fires: `FuturesUnordered`, `FuturesOrdered`, `select_all`, `JoinSet`. N concurrent calls pushed into one are each polled once and queue their tasks; the turns resolve the tasks into result channels nobody re-reads; the closure stays pending; `run_concurrent` reaches `Outcome::Idle` with no pending work (`src/store/store_context.rs:257-271`) and parks forever, with no error and no timeout. Wasmtime's `call_concurrent` awaits a oneshot receiver, which is waker-correct.

Keep an `Option<Waker>` beside the value in the result channel and in the failure slot; store the waker on every `Pending`; wake it from the task's resolve (`src/concurrency/task.rs`, `Task::resolve`) and from every writer of the failure slot in `func.rs`, including the borrow failure a synchronous task can raise after it resolved.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A `run_concurrent` closure that pushes several `call_concurrent` futures into a waker-gated combinator (a `FuturesUnordered`, or a test-local combinator that re-polls a child only after its waker fires) completes with every result on both targets.
- [ ] A concurrent call that resolves and then fails on the borrows the guest still owes wakes its caller with that failure.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green on both targets with the conformance summary unchanged.
