## What to build

Wasmtime 50's event loop yields to the host executor after 128 work items
without a yield (`e2a58746c`). The polyfill's driver continues while it makes
progress (`concurrency/driver.rs:149`). In the browser, a long run of
high-priority work, such as a callback that keeps cancelling host subtasks,
holds the page.

After 128 turns without a yield, the driver returns `Pending` and wakes itself.
In the browser the wake crosses a macrotask through `MessageChannel`, as the
yield wake does, so the page can render and handle input. The owner wants to
revisit the budget and the hop in the PDD for guest interruption and resource
limits (050629).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] After 128 turns without a yield, the driver returns to the host on both
      targets, and the order that a guest sees does not change.
- [ ] Ports of Wasmtime's `run_concurrent_yields_during_high_priority_work`
      and `guest_cancellation_loop_does_not_starve_tokio` pass on both
      targets.
- [ ] PDD018 states the budget.
