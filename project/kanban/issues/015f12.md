## What to build
Generate Wasmtime's `{World}Pre<T>` and `{World}Indices` (`crates/component-macro/tests/expanded/smoke.rs:9-129` at `v49.0.0-rc.1`) once the polyfill has a pre-instantiated component (`InstancePre`). `{World}Indices` resolves the export indices and runs the type check once per component; `{World}Pre` instantiates many times from one link.

PDD028 emits neither because the polyfill has no `InstancePre`. The design of `InstancePre` belongs to 2042de.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `wcmp::bindgen!` emits `{World}Pre` with `new`, `engine`, `instance_pre`, and `instantiate_async`, and `{World}Indices` with `new` and `load`, in Wasmtime's shapes.
- [ ] A test counts type checks across 100 instantiations through one `{World}Pre`: the type check of exports and host imports runs once.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green.
