## What to build
`TypedFunc::call` and `call_concurrent` turn their arguments into `Vec<Val>` and read their results back from `Val` (`src/instance/typed_func.rs:90, 117, 126-129`), and `lift` sizes a `Vec<Val>` by the guest's length bounded only by the memory size (`src/abi/lift.rs:45`), so a `list<u8>` costs about forty bytes per element on the way through. A payload of 100 MiB becomes roughly 4 GiB of `Val`, which is the whole address space of the polyfill in the browser: an out-of-memory abort rather than an error. Wasmtime's typed `Vec<u8>` is a copy, and its untyped path charges fuel per element. Give `ComponentValue` a direct lower and lift for numeric `Vec<T>` and `String` that never builds a `Val` per element, which is also the seam the host-binding derive will need, and bound the untyped path: the copy budget PDD021 defines once it lands, or a store-level limit with a structured cause until then, whichever the owner prefers. Measure with the benchmark suite and record the numbers on this card.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A `TypedFunc` round trip of a `Vec<u8>` and a `String` allocates no `Val` per element, proved by a counting allocator or an access hook, on both targets.
- [ ] The untyped path refuses a list whose lifted size exceeds the bound with a structured cause instead of allocating, proved by a test with a large guest list.
- [ ] The benchmark numbers before and after are recorded on this card for both targets.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green on both targets with the conformance summary unchanged.
