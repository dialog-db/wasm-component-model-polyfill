## What to build
`TypedFunc::call` and `call_concurrent` turn their arguments into `Vec<Val>` and read their results back from `Val` (`src/instance/typed_func.rs:90, 117, 126-129`), and `lift` sizes a `Vec<Val>` by the guest's length bounded only by the memory size (`src/abi/lift.rs:45`), so a `list<u8>` costs about forty bytes per element on the way through. A payload of 100 MiB becomes roughly 4 GiB of `Val`, which is the whole address space of the polyfill in the browser: an out-of-memory abort rather than an error. Wasmtime's typed `Vec<u8>` is a copy, and its untyped path charges fuel per element. Give `ComponentValue` a direct lower and lift for numeric `Vec<T>` and `String` that never builds a `Val` per element, the way Wasmtime's `Lower` and `Lift` for byte slices are one copy into or out of guest memory; it is also the seam the host-binding derive will need. Payloads of hundreds of megabytes are an expected use of this polyfill, so the target is one host-side copy per crossing, which is the floor the runtime layer's copy-in, copy-out memory API sets; a direct view into guest memory needs an upstream runtime-layer change that PDD021 names as the optimization surface, and this card points at it rather than building it. The bound on the untyped path is card `d0d703`'s. Measure with the benchmark suite and record the numbers on this card.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A `TypedFunc` round trip of a `Vec<u8>` and a `String` allocates no `Val` per element, proved by a counting allocator or an access hook, on both targets.
- [x] A `TypedFunc` round trip of a `Vec<u8>` of several hundred megabytes completes natively with one host-side copy per direction, proved by a test that counts substrate accesses or allocations.
- [x] The benchmark numbers before and after are recorded on this card for both targets.
- [x] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green on both targets with the conformance summary unchanged.
