## What to build
Add a per-store stack of call scopes. Push at trampoline entry and pop at exit. Count borrows lowered into the guest, decrement on a borrow drop, record lenders on borrow lifts, and undo lends at exit. A non-zero count at exit surfaces `AbiCause::OutstandingBorrows { count }`. Make the `enter-sync-call` and `exit-sync-call` adapter intrinsics push and validate the same scope. Un-stub `it_rejects_a_host_call_that_leaves_outstanding_borrows`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `it_rejects_a_host_call_that_leaves_outstanding_borrows` passes on every target with a count of 1.
- [ ] A borrow dropped inside the call leaves the owning handle live after the call.
- [ ] The corpus file `resources/borrows.wast` moves its borrow-scope directives out of the expected-failures list.
