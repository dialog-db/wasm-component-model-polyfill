## What to build

Follow-up from the Opus 5 re-review of the scheduler error variant. The deadlock and cannot-block messages are copied from Wasmtime 48 as string literals in `SchedulerCause` and again in its tests, so a `wasmtime` bump that rewords a trap leaves both green while the conformance corpus silently stops matching. `wasmtime-environ` is already a direct dependency; add a test per message that compares the rendered cause against the crate's own `Trap` rendering, for example that `Trap::AsyncDeadlock.to_string()` ends with `SchedulerCause::Deadlock.to_string()` and the same for `Trap::CannotBlockSyncTask` and `SchedulerCause::CannotBlock`. Keep the literals; the test is the pin.
