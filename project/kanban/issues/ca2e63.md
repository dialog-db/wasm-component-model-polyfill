## What to build
The review of card `7d72a0` accepted the poll scope around a concurrent registration's closure and found the tests cover only one of the shapes the card names. The card's motivation is a host task inside a synchronous call, but every new test in `tests/baseline_async_lower.rs` is an asynchronous lower whose future is ready on its first poll; `tests/baseline_sync_lower.rs` already registers `func_wrap_concurrent` and needs one test where the closure reaches the store before its `async move` block. Two more shapes are unproved: a body that stays pending across turns after the closure's reach (a closure reach followed by a later reach from the pending body, proving the two scopes do not interfere), and the no-turn path — a concurrent import called from a destructor run by `Store::resource_drop`, where the closure runs under the noop waker and the host task must still be polled by the next turn. Add the three tests on both targets.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A `func_wrap_concurrent` closure behind a synchronous lower reads the host data before its `async move` block, proved on both targets.
- [ ] A closure reach followed by a reach from the same body in a later turn both succeed, proved by a test whose body stays pending across at least one turn.
- [ ] A concurrent import called from a destructor outside any turn answers on a later turn, proved by a test.
- [ ] `lint` passes and `tests all` is green on both targets with the conformance summary unchanged.
